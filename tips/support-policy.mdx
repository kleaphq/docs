## Support Access

| Channel | Open to | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Documentation** | Everyone | Self-service guides and troubleshooting |
| **Chat on kleap.co** | Everyone | Your own account, apps, credits and billing |
| **Discord** | Everyone | Community help, product questions, and ideas |
| **Email** | Everyone | Accounts, billing, security, and detailed technical issues |

Every channel is open on every plan. **A human answering is included from Pro**, and triaged first on Business. On Free and Launch the assistant helps as far as it can — we will not promise you a human who is not coming.

**Except for money, data and our own mistakes**, which are never gated by a plan: billing, a refund, a double charge, a failed payment, lost data, a bug on our side, or an account change only we can make reaches a human **on any plan, including Free**.

## Reporting Bugs

### What to Include

1. **Description** - What happened vs what you expected
2. **Steps to reproduce** - How to trigger the bug
3. **Screenshots/recordings** - Visual evidence
4. **Error messages** - Copy exact text
5. **Browser/device** - Where you saw the issue

### Where to Report

- **Chat on kleap.co** - Fastest, and it already knows your account and apps
- **Discord** - For quick community feedback
- **Email** - For confirmed bugs
- **GitHub Issues** - For our open source repositories ([kleaphq](https://github.com/kleaphq))

## Feature Requests

We love hearing ideas! Share them:

1. **Discord** - Discuss with other builders
2. **Email** - Straight to the founder

Include:
- What you want to build
- Why existing features don't work
- How it would help your workflow

## Availability and Response

Kleap is founder-run. The support assistant in the chat on kleap.co answers at any hour, but **human support is one person** — the founder — so there are no staffed support hours and no guaranteed response time. "Priority" on Business means your message is triaged first, never that it is answered within a set time.

Leave your message on any channel and it will be read. A site that is down or a failed payment is looked at first; everything else is answered in turn, and a weekend or a busy week can stretch that out. Contractual support terms, if any, are stated in the relevant agreement.

## Account Issues

### Can't Log In

1. Check email/password are correct
2. Try password reset
3. Clear browser cookies
4. Contact support if still stuck

### Billing Questions

- **Email**: hello@kleap.co
- Include your account email
- Describe the issue clearly

### Account Deletion

To delete your account:

1. Export your data if needed
2. Email hello@kleap.co
3. We&apos;ll confirm the request and explain the applicable deletion timeline
