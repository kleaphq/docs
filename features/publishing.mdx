Kleap hosts published web projects for you — one click gives you a production URL with no build step to manage.

## Publishing is a deliberate action

Your site is **not public until you publish it**. Building and refining in the editor changes nothing for visitors — that way an unfinished site never goes live under your name.

When you're ready, click **Publish** (top-right in the builder). Kleap builds your site and deploys it to a permanent URL.

<Frame caption="Open the Publish panel, check the address, then click Publish">
  <img src="/images/tutorials/publish.webp" alt="Kleap Publish panel in English showing the site address and Publish button" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Later changes are **not** pushed live automatically. Each time you want the public site to catch up with your edits, click **Update** — the same button.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Publishing does not consume AI credits. Publishing availability, hosting, and published-project limits depend on your plan; see [current pricing](https://kleap.co/pricing).
</Info>

## Your Production URL

Once published, your site is live at a permanent URL:

```
https://your-app-name.kleap.io
```

This URL:

- Remains available until you unpublish it, subject to the [Terms of Service](https://kleap.co/tos)
- Is **publicly accessible**
- Supports **HTTPS** automatically
- Is served on a **global edge network** for fast loading worldwide
- Can be **shared** with anyone

## Preview vs Production

| Aspect | Preview | Production |
|--------|---------|------------|
| Where | In the Kleap editor | `your-app.kleap.io` |
| Intended use | Building session | Live visitors |
| Access | You (and your team) | Public |
| Purpose | Building and testing | Live visitors |

<Info>
  The preview is a real build of your current files, so what you see is what visitors get.
</Info>

## Updating Your Site

After the initial publish:

1. Make changes in Kleap (or ask the AI)
2. Review the updated preview
3. Click **Update** when you want the new version to go live

<Frame caption="Publish and Update use the same top-right deployment panel">
  <img src="/images/tutorials/editor-toolbar.webp" alt="Kleap builder toolbar in English with the Publish control at the top right" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Each publish creates a new version. Use [Version History](/features/version-history) to roll back anytime.
</Tip>

## Deployment Status

Track your deployment:

- **Building** — your site is compiling
- **Deploying** — uploading to the edge
- **Ready** — live and accessible
- **Error** — build failed (see the error and re-try)

If a deployment fails, the error is shown in the chat. Ask the AI to fix it, then publish again when the preview is ready.

## Unpublishing

To take your site offline:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Choose **Unpublish**
3. Your site is no longer publicly accessible

<Info>
  Unpublishing doesn't delete your site — you can republish anytime.
</Info>
