## Before Contacting Support

To help us resolve your issue faster, please:

1. **Check the docs** - Your question might be answered here
2. **Search Discord** - Someone may have asked the same question
3. **Try troubleshooting** - See our [Troubleshooting Guide](/tips/troubleshooting)

## What to Include

When reaching out, include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the issue">
    What are you trying to do? What happens instead?
  </Step>
  <Step title="App details">
    Which app is affected? Share the app name or ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Steps to reproduce">
    How can we see the same issue?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Screenshots or videos">
    Visuals help us understand faster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Error messages">
    Copy any error messages you see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Support Availability

All three channels are open on every plan. What your plan changes is whether a **human** answers.

| Channel | Open to | Best for |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Chat on kleap.co | Everyone | Anything about your own account, apps, credits or billing |
| Discord | Everyone | Quick questions, discussions, and feature ideas |
| Email | Everyone | Accounts, billing, security, and detailed technical issues |

**Human support is included from Pro.** On Business your message is triaged first, ahead of Pro. On Free and Launch the assistant helps you as far as it can and you are welcome on Discord and by email, but a human reply is not part of the plan — we would rather say so than let you wait for one.

<Note>
  **Money, data and our own bugs are never gated by a plan.** A billing question, a refund, a double charge, a failed payment, lost data, a bug on our side that burned your credits, or an account change only we can make reaches a human **on any plan, including Free**. That is us fixing our own side, not support.
</Note>

Kleap is founder-run: human support is **one person**, not a rota, so there are no staffed support hours and no guaranteed response time. A site that is down or a failed payment is looked at first; everything else is answered in turn, and a weekend or a busy week can stretch that out. "Priority" means triaged first, never answered within a set time. Contractual support terms, if any, are stated in the relevant agreement.

## Feature Requests

Have an idea to make Kleap better?

1. Post it in our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/WhQrT8RneY), or email **hello@kleap.co**
2. Say what you are trying to build and why the current features do not get you there
3. It reaches the founder directly — there is no committee in between

## Bug Reports

Found a bug?

1. Tell us in the chat on kleap.co, on [Discord](https://discord.gg/WhQrT8RneY), or at **hello@kleap.co**
2. Include steps to reproduce
3. Share screenshots if possible
4. We'll investigate and follow up
