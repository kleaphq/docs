Kleap uses a credit-based system. Credits are consumed when you use the AI to build and edit projects. Publishing actions do not consume AI credits; publishing availability, hosting, and published-project limits depend on your plan.

<Info>
  Plans and prices below are current entry points. For the live, up-to-date pricing and credit sliders, visit [kleap.co/pricing](https://kleap.co/pricing).
</Info>

## Pricing Plans

| Plan | Price | Best for | Monthly AI credits |
|------|-------|----------|--------------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Trying Kleap, small sites | 5 free credits per day |
| **Launch** | $9/mo (or $99/yr) | Publishing with your own domain | Hosting only — keeps the 5 free daily credits |
| **Pro** | from $25/mo | Growing businesses | from 100 credits/mo (scalable) |
| **Business** | from $50/mo | Scaling teams | from 100 credits/mo (scalable) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Large teams | Custom — [contact us](mailto:hello@kleap.co) |

### How Pro & Business scale

Pro and Business aren't fixed tiers — you pick how many monthly AI credits you need with a slider, and the price scales with it (from 100 credits up to 10,000+). **Business** includes everything in **Pro** plus team features: **SSO**, **design templates**, **private projects**, and the option to **opt out of data training**.

Paid plans bill **monthly or yearly** — yearly billing is discounted. See [kleap.co/pricing](https://kleap.co/pricing) for exact annual savings.

<Info>
  Every plan — including Free — includes **5 free AI credits per day**. **Launch** is a hosting plan: it unlocks a custom domain and always-on hosting, and keeps your daily free credits, but doesn't add a monthly credit pool.
</Info>

## How Credits Work

### Free daily credits

Every user can claim **5 free credits** once every 24 hours.

- **Claim frequency**: once per 24 hours
- **Credits per claim**: 5
- **Free-plan cap**: up to 30 claims per month

### Paid monthly credits

Paid plans add a monthly pool of AI credits on top of the daily free ones.

- **Paid credits roll over** — they never expire
- Free daily credits are used first, then your paid balance

### Credit consumption

Credits are **usage-based**, not a flat cost per message. A short tweak costs a fraction of a credit; a large, complex build costs more. As a rough guide:

| Action | Estimated Credits |
|--------|-------------------|
| Small text or style change | Under 1 credit |
| New section or component | 1–3 credits |
| Full page creation | 3–8 credits |
| Large, complex feature | 8+ credits |

### Credit top-ups

Paid subscribers can buy a one-off pack without changing plans from [kleap.co/credits](https://kleap.co/credits). Sign in with the subscribed account to see the available packs:

| Pack | Credits |
|------|---------|
| Starter | 50 |
| Popular | 150 |
| Pro | 400 |
| Max | 1,000 |

Prices are shown in your own currency on the page. Top-ups add to your balance and leave your plan untouched.

Your **first paid subscription** also includes a **+50% bonus** on that plan's monthly credits.

### What happens when you run out?

- Your published sites keep working normally
- You can't make new AI edits until you get more credits
- Options: wait for your next daily credits, upgrade your plan, or earn referral credits

## Referral Program

Earn bonus credits by inviting friends:

| Who | Credits Earned |
|-----|----------------|
| **Your friend (referee)** | +5 credits, immediately on signup |
| **You (referrer)** | +10 credits once your friend actually uses Kleap — they publish a site and come back on a later day |
| **You (referrer)** | +100 credits if your friend subscribes to a paid plan, released after a 7-day verification period |

<Note>
  A signup on its own earns the referrer nothing. The +10 is released when your friend genuinely
  gets going, which is what keeps the program honest for everyone.

  Free referral rewards are capped at 100 credits over the lifetime of your account, with a maximum
  of 3 grants per 30 days. Paid-conversion rewards are outside that cap.
</Note>

### How to Refer

1. Go to [kleap.co/referral](https://kleap.co/referral)
2. Copy your unique referral link
3. Share it with friends
4. Your friend gets their 5 credits straight away; yours arrive once they start building

## Free Actions

These never consume credits:

- Viewing and browsing your sites
- Publishing or updating an eligible web project (plan access and hosting limits still apply)
- Connecting an eligible custom domain (plan access still applies)
- Editing text directly in the preview
- Generating images
- Viewing version history and the code editor
- Managing account and team settings

## Billing FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    Your sites keep working. You just can't make new AI edits until your next daily credits arrive, you buy a top-up, or you upgrade.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do paid credits expire?">
    No. Paid monthly credits roll over and never expire. Free daily claims add to your balance on top.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. Cancel from Settings and keep access until the end of your billing period.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monthly or yearly?">
    Both. Yearly billing is discounted versus monthly — see [kleap.co/pricing](https://kleap.co/pricing) for exact savings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I claim daily credits?">
    Open your dashboard and claim once every 24 hours.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
